# Registration

## Create Your Account

### Email Registration

1. Visit [NexusQuant](https://nexusquant.io) and click **Register**
2. Enter your **email address**
3. Choose a **username**
4. Create a **password** (at least 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers)
5. Click **Register**
6. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to activate your account

![NexusQuant registration page](/images/auth/nexusquant-register.png)

::: tip
If you don't see the verification email, check your spam/junk folder. The email is sent from NexusQuant's system.
:::

### Google Sign-In

You can also register using your Google account for a faster setup:

![Google Sign-In button](/images/auth/nexusquant-google-signin.png)

1. Click **Sign in with Google** on the registration page
2. Select your Google account and authorize NexusQuant
3. Your account is created automatically — no email verification needed

## Referral Registration

If you received a referral link from an existing user:

1. Use the referral link to open the registration page
2. The referral code is automatically applied
3. Complete registration as described above
4. You'll receive **10% off** your first Pro subscription

## After Registration

Once your account is created:

1. **[Connect an exchange](/exchange-setup)** — Add your exchange API key to start trading
2. **[Choose a strategy](/getting-started/quick-start#step-3-choose-and-activate-a-strategy)** — Select from funding arbitrage, webhook signals, or smart DCA
3. **Explore the dashboard** — Familiarize yourself with the overview, positions, and trades pages

## Account Security

- Use a **strong, unique password** for your NexusQuant account
- If using Google Sign-In, ensure your Google account has **2FA enabled**
- You can change your password anytime from the **Profile** page
