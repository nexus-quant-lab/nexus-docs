# Registration

## Create Your Account

1. Visit [NexusQuant](https://nexusquant.io) and click **Register**
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a strong password (at least 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers)
4. Complete email verification by clicking the link sent to your inbox

## After Registration

Once your account is created:

1. **[Connect an exchange](/exchange-setup)** — Add your exchange API key to start trading
2. **[Choose a strategy](/getting-started/quick-start#step-3-choose-and-activate-a-strategy)** — Select from funding arbitrage, webhook signals, or smart DCA
3. **Configure risk settings** — Set your position limits and safety parameters

## Referral Program

If you were invited by an existing user, make sure to use their referral link during registration for potential benefits.
