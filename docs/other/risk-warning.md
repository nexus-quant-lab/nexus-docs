# Risk Warning

::: danger
Trading involves substantial risk and may not be suitable for all investors.
:::

## Key Risks

1. **Market Risk**: Prices can move rapidly and significantly.
2. **Liquidity Risk**: Some assets may have low liquidity.
3. **Leverage Risk**: Using leverage can amplify both gains and losses.
4. **Technical Risk**: Platform technical issues may occur.

## Risk Management Tips

- Only trade with funds you can afford to lose
- Use stop-loss orders
- Diversify your portfolio
- Stay informed about market conditions
- Do your own research before making any trades
