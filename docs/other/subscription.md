# Subscription Plans

NexusQuant offers a free tier for basic usage and a **Pro Subscription** to unlock advanced features and higher trading limits.

![Subscription page](/images/settings/nexusquant-subscription.png)

## Plan Comparison

| Feature | Free | Pro Monthly | Pro Yearly |
|---|------|----------|----------|

| **Exchange Connections** | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Strategy Limit** | 1 per type | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Webhook Automation** | Basic | Full (SL/TP, templates) | Full (SL/TP, templates) |
| **Funding Rate Arb** | Monitoring only | Auto-execute | Auto-execute |
| **Smart DCA (ahr999)** | View only | Auto-execute | Auto-execute |
| **Telegram Notifications** | Basic | Priority Alerts | Priority Alerts |

## How to Subscribe

1. Navigate to the **Subscription** page in your dashboard.
2. Select your preferred plan (Monthly or Yearly).
3. If you were referred by a friend, a **10% discount** will be automatically applied to your first payment.
4. Click **Pay with Crypto** (powered by NOWPayments).
5. Choose your preferred cryptocurrency and send the exact amount.
6. Once the transaction is confirmed on the blockchain, your account will be upgraded to Pro automatically.

## Supported Payment Networks

We support 350+ cryptocurrencies via NOWPayments. For low fees, we recommend using:
- **TRC20** (Tron)
- **BEP20** (BNB Smart Chain)
- **Solana**
- **Polygon**

::: tip Security Note
Always double-check the wallet address and network before sending funds. NexusQuant will never proactively ask for your private keys or request transfers to private addresses via social apps.
:::

New users who register via a referral link are eligible for a **10% discount** on their very first subscription payment. This discount is calculated and applied automatically at checkout.
