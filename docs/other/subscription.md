# Subscription Plans

NexusQuant offers a free tier for basic usage and a **Pro Subscription** to unlock advanced features and higher trading limits.

## Pricing Plans

| Plan | Price (USDT) | Duration | Features |
|------|--------------|----------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Lifetime | 1 Active Strategy, Standard Support |
| **Pro Monthly** | $29 | 30 Days | Unlimited Strategies, Priority Support, Early Access |
| **Pro Yearly** | $290 | 365 Days | Same as Pro + **17% Discount** ($24/month) |

## How to Subscribe

1. Navigate to the **Subscription** page in your dashboard.
2. Select your preferred plan (Monthly or Yearly).
3. If you were referred by a friend, a **10% discount** will be automatically applied to your first payment.
4. Choose your payment method (powered by NOWPayments).
5. Send the exact amount of USDT to the provided wallet address.
6. Once the transaction is confirmed on the blockchain, your account will be upgraded to Pro automatically.

## Supported Payment Networks

We support USDT payments on the following networks:
- **TRC20** (Tron) - *Recommended for low fees*
- **ERC20** (Ethereum)
- **BEP20** (BNB Smart Chain)
- **Solana**

::: tip Security Note
Always double-check the wallet address and network before sending funds. NexusQuant will never ask for your private keys.
:::

## Referral Discount
New users who register via a referral link are eligible for a **10% discount** on their very first subscription payment. This discount is applied automatically at checkout.
