# Affiliate Program

Share NexusQuant with your audience and earn competitive commissions on every subscription they purchase.

## Commission Structure

Our Affiliate Program features a tiered commission structure based on the number of active affiliates in the system:

| Tier | Slots | Commission Rate |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| **Tier 1** | First 20 Affiliates | **40%** |
| **Tier 2** | Next 30 Affiliates | **30%** |
| **Tier 3** | All subsequent | **20%** |

*Note: Your tier is locked in at the time your affiliate application is approved.*

## General Referral vs. Affiliate

| Feature | General Referral | Affiliate Partner |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Reward** | 7 Bonus Pro Days | Cash (USDT) Commission |
| **User Benefit** | 10% First-time Discount | 10% First-time Discount |
| **Custom Code** | No (System generated) | **Yes** (Customizable) |
| **Approval** | Automatic | Manual Review Required |

## How to Become an Affiliate

1. **Register** an account on NexusQuant.
2. Navigate to the **Affiliate** section in your dashboard.
3. Click **Apply for Affiliate**.
4. (Optional) Choose a **Custom Referral Code** (e.g., your brand name).
5. Submit your application.
6. Our team will review your application within 24-48 hours.

## Payouts

- Commissions are earned in **USDT**.
- You can track your earnings and referrals in real-time on the Affiliate Dashboard.
- Withdrawals can be requested once you reach the minimum threshold (default: 50 USDT).

## Rules

- Self-referrals are strictly prohibited and will result in account suspension.
- Do not engage in spamming or deceptive marketing practices.
- NexusQuant reserves the right to adjust commission rates for new sign-ups.
